Log in under Manage Accounts and the artists you follow on Bluesky, Mastodon and pixiv show up here by themselves. Or create a card and add the places an artist posts. Sourcerer downloads every picture and video from there into your library – and on every sync, only what is new.

    New artist card
    Sources

    Paste any page of a profile – its media tab, a single post – and Sourcerer finds the profile behind it. Leave the fields of sites the artist is not on empty. Only sites you are logged in to are listed – Manage Accounts adds more.

    Folder

    Left empty, the card gets its own folder in your library. Renaming the card or choosing another folder moves the files along.

    Auto-download

    On: a sync of this card also downloads what it finds, and Automatic sync does. Off: a sync only looks for new posts (the card says how many are waiting) and you download when you want.

    Master

    Manage your accounts

    The login works. It works, but the site rests for a while. It ran out – log in again. Not logged in. Sourcerer keeps what proves a login on this computer, never your password, and checks every login at each start.

    Login files: each login is one file in that folder. Copy them to keep your logins; to log in again without typing, put them back and press Check all. They hold cookies and tokens, so whoever has them can use your logins – keep them private.

    Sourcerer open in the browser instead of its own window? Export the site's cookies with a browser extension such as "Get cookies.txt LOCALLY" and use Import….

    Settings
    TrackImage

    Your local, danbooru-style image library: tags, duplicates, a gallery you can search. No account, no cloud, no telemetry. What Sourcerer downloads fits right in.

    Library

    Default library folder

    A card without a folder of its own gets one in here. Add this folder to TrackImage and new files show up there by themselves.

    File names

    Sync

    Add the accounts I follow

    After a login, and every half day, the accounts you follow on Bluesky, Mastodon and pixiv join the list as artists. Nothing is read or downloaded for them until you open one or do something with it.

    Look for new posts at every start

    Every time Sourcerer is opened it looks at the cards with auto-download, one after the other, and downloads what they find (the same as Refresh). Other cards are looked at when you open them. Sourcerer checks for its own updates at every start anyway.

    Automatic sync

    Syncs the cards with auto-download on its own while Sourcerer is open: it looks for new posts and downloads them.

    Every
    Parallel downloads

    Files downloaded at the same time. More is faster on a fast line; a very high number only helps with many artists at once, and some sites then slow down or refuse you.

    Sites & network

    Sourcerer reads public posts through each site's official interface (the Mastodon API, Bluesky's AT Protocol), as a known app: it names itself in every request and never pretends to be a browser. These limits keep it well below what the sites allow, so an account is never mistaken for an attack.

    Connections per site

    Requests to one site at the same time. Sites that are touchy about it (pixiv, Patreon, logins) keep their own lower limit whatever this says.

    Pause between requests

    Per site. Longer is gentler, shorter is faster.

    Gap between artists on a cookie login

    pixiv, X, Patreon and forums are read with your logged-in session, which is what gets locked when it looks like a robot. This is the pause between two artists on the same such site: careful 20-90 s, normal 10-40 s, fast 3-12 s. The one you opened in Browse always goes first, with a short pause. Bluesky, Mastodon, Misskey and boorus are not affected.

    When a site says "too many requests", Sourcerer waits as long as the site asks before it tries again. Automatic syncs start at a slightly different minute each time.

    Appearance

    Theme
    Picture quality in the grid

    Sharp shows every picture in the largest size the site offers below the original (pixiv's 1200 px, X's large, Bluesky's full size) from the start. Quick shows the small ones first: lighter on a slow line, but visibly softer. Reopen the artist to apply.

    Site mark on the pictures

    Shows which site a picture comes from, in its corner. In “All artists” the artist's name stays.

    Security

    Master password

    It stops other people from using Sourcerer's window. Your files in Userdata are not encrypted, and a sync that is set to run on its own keeps running while Sourcerer is locked.

    Reset Sourcerer

    Starts over as if it had just been installed: logs out of every site (and tells the site, where it has a way), forgets all artists, their sources and the settings, removes the master password, the profile pictures, caches and log files, and restarts. Your downloaded pictures stay in the library folder, and so do the backups of the updater; delete those yourself if you want them gone. This cannot be undone.
